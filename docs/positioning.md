## 1. What Publeca is

Publeca is a **full-stack growth consulting firm**. We find where a company's next stage of
growth will come from, then **execute it ourselves** — strategy, brand, content, paid media,
SEO and AEO/GEO, conversion tracking and analytics, product marketing, web and software
development, and AI solutions.

What makes us different (use these as the backbone of the value story):

1. **Strategy and execution in one firm.** Most companies buy strategy from consultants (who
   hand over a deck), marketing from agencies (who execute without the plan), and software
   from developers (who build without knowing the market). Nobody owns the result. We do.
2. **Forward-deployed growth executives.** A senior growth operator embedded with the client
   (on-site or remote) who identifies the right opportunities, owns the growth plan, and
   directs our specialist teams to execute it. The client gets senior growth leadership
   without the cost and risk of a full-time C-level hire.
3. **Our own departments, under one roof** (use these exact names):
   - **Growth Consulting** — diagnosis, opportunity sizing, growth planning, forward-deployed executives.
   - **Marketing & Brand** — branding, content, social media ads, Google Ads, SEO, AEO/GEO, product marketing.
   - **Engineering** — web development, software development, AI solutions.
   - **Analytics & Data Science** — conversion tracking and analytics for websites **and apps**, attribution, dashboards, modelling.
   - **Events** — our original arm: event marketing, event photo/video, and the Publeca ticketing platform.
4. **Measured from day one.** Our own analytics & data science team sets up tracking before
   spend scales, so every decision is tied to revenue, not vanity metrics.
5. **No equity, no lock-in mindset.** Founders get a growth team without giving away stake.

Location: Sri Lanka. We work on-site and remotely. **Do not** claim specific clients,
countries served, team size, years in business, awards or partner certifications.

## 2. How we work (canonical method — reuse these names)

1. **Diagnose** — find where growth will actually come from: market, customers, funnel,
   unit economics, data quality.
2. **Prioritize** — rank opportunities by financial impact, effort and risk; agree a 90-day plan.
3. **Deploy** — a forward-deployed growth executive leads; our specialist teams execute.
4. **Measure** — tracking and analytics from day one; plain-language read-outs every week.
5. **Scale** — double down on what works; build the systems, software and AI that make it repeatable.

## 3. Audiences (segment pages at `/for/[slug]`)

### Directors & CEOs — `ceos`
- **Who:** run (and usually own) an established company; want the next stage of growth;
  little technical knowledge; tired of agencies and jargon.
- **Core value:** clarity on exactly where the next growth comes from, and a senior team that
  delivers it — revenue and market share they can see, explained in plain English.
- **Offer:** Growth Consulting + Forward-Deployed Growth Executives — from identifying the
  right opportunity to executing it.
- **Packages (names can be refined, keep the shape):**
  1. *Growth Diagnostic* — fixed fee, ~3 weeks: opportunity map, financial model of growth
     levers, 90-day plan presented to leadership. **Recommended start.**
  2. *Forward-Deployed Growth Executive* — monthly: embedded senior operator + our specialist
     teams; weekly plain-language read-outs; monthly board-ready report.
  3. *Growth Advisory* — monthly, light-touch: strategy session, KPI review, oversight of
     existing agencies/teams.

### Technical founders — `technical-founders`
- **Who:** built the product; strong on engineering; want to grow and scale **without
  giving equity to a business/marketing co-founder**.
- **Core value:** the go-to-market muscle of a growth co-founder — positioning, demand,
  tracking, product marketing — **while keeping 100% of the equity**.
- **Packages:**
  1. *Go-to-Market Sprint* — fixed scope, ~6 weeks: positioning, ICP, conversion tracking,
     launch campaign. **Recommended start.**
  2. *Fractional Growth Team* — monthly: growth lead + marketing + analytics, no equity.
  3. *Scale Partner* — quarterly: full-funnel growth, product marketing, conversion
     engineering, AI automation.

### Investors & VCs — `investors`
- **Who:** investors/VCs (and portfolio/platform teams) who want a growth team they can point
  at portfolio companies.
- **Tone: understated, very low push.** Write like an operator sharing a perspective, not a
  vendor. No urgency, no superlatives, no exclamation marks. Let the substance sell.
- **Core value:** a senior operating growth team portfolio companies can call on — faster
  than a hire, more accountable than an agency — with reporting a partner can read in minutes.
- **Packages:**
  1. *Portfolio Growth Review* — ~2 weeks per company: independent go-to-market assessment,
     scorecard, prioritized fixes, read-out to founder and partner. **Recommended start.**
  2. *Embedded Growth Pod* — a quarter inside a portfolio company: growth lead + specialists.
  3. *Commercial Diligence Support* — pre-investment: market, positioning, funnel and
     unit-economics sanity check.
- **CTAs:** "Request a sample portfolio brief" (email) and "Or simply compare notes" (WhatsApp).
- Subtle placement: listed in "Who we help", never pushed in banners or pop-ups.

## 5. Events arm (`/events`)

Kept as an arm. Differentiator: **the only place to build an event page with paid-booking
conversion tracking built in** — Meta & Google learn who actually buys, so cost per ticket
falls. Two paths: done-for-you event marketing, or self-serve ticketing platform
(custom event pages, PayHere + Koko/Mintpay BNPL with the host's own merchant accounts,
QR e-tickets, door scanning, assigned seating, Meta/Google conversion tracking).

  It never outputs a score, forecast or number.
